Critical methodological finding
-------------------------------
Equal generation counts do NOT imply equal computational search budgets in this codebase.
The hybrids perform several full-population objective recalculations inside each generation,
while the standalone comparators perform approximately one offspring evaluation per population
member per generation. Therefore a Q1 comparison based only on an equal number of generations
would materially favor algorithms that consume more objective evaluations.

Required action before main experiments
---------------------------------------
The definitive Q1 runner must support a fixed objective-evaluation budget and log both:
1. objective evaluations; and
2. wall-clock runtime.

The paper may additionally report equal-generation convergence as a secondary diagnostic, but
the primary state-of-the-art comparison should be evaluation-budget controlled.
